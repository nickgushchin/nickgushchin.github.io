My class as vectors

After the lecture on inner products and nearest neighbours, I turned my class of 55 (54 classmates and me) into vectors (35 features each, from public info only) and compared every pair with the inner product. Bigger number = more alike. All classmates are anonymised as P01–P54.

How to read it: each cell is the similarity of the row person and the column person. Cosine is the inner product after scaling every vector to length 1, so it runs from −1 (opposite) to +1 (identical). The raw inner product is the unscaled sum of feature × feature. Features are weighted towards hands-on skills, seniority and activity. A classroom experiment, not a judgement of anyone.
