What we cover

From how AI thinks to an agent that works for you

We start with the ideas behind the tools, so the rest makes sense. Then most of the evening is building, on your own account, with your own tasks.

01 · What AI is

From a formula to GPT

How a model learns, from a regression line to a neural net that predicts the next word. A short history from 1956 to today, and the map of models: frontier and open source.

02 · From tool to agent

What an agent really is

Three levels: tool, assistant, agent. What an agent is made of: interface, server, model, data, skills and tools. Real agents, including the one that runs my week.

03 · Speaking with AI

Prompts, then skills that fix prompts

Why ChatGPT behaves the way it does, the five parts of a good prompt, and two skills that improve your prompts and your texts for you.

04 · Build your agent

Claude or ChatGPT, step by step

Where skills, projects and memory live. Then we build: a profile from your CV, your email connected, a morning brief that runs on a schedule.

05 · AI in companies

How industry adopts it safely

What is coming in industry, the AI pyramid, how to test AI tools for risks, and one controlled platform that a whole company can use.

06 · Close

Use it tomorrow or lose it

Agents in daily life, why we forget most of a training within a week, and how to keep yours running. You get the take-home pack.
